Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 24, 2026

Google Ready ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Google Ready - FAANG DSA Prep ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our service:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address to identify your account and sync your data across devices.
  • DSA Practice Data: Problem logs, progress records, goals, and statistics that you enter into the App.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous app usage statistics to improve performance (collected via Firebase Analytics).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's core functionality (tracking your DSA practice progress)
  • To sync your data across your devices
  • To improve the App's features and user experience
  • To communicate important updates (only when necessary)

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase services, which comply with industry-standard security practices. We use encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Access to your data is restricted to your Google Account only.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Google Firebase: For data storage and authentication (Google's privacy policy applies)
  • Google AdMob: For serving ads within the app (Google's privacy policy applies)
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our legal rights

5. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data to show personalized ads based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as you use the App. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by:

  • Contacting us at privacy@googleready.app
  • Or by deleting your account within the App settings

Upon deletion request, all your personal data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Data portability

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
